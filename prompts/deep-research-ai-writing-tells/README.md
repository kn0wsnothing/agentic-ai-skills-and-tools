# deep-research-ai-writing-tells

A ready-to-run deep research prompt for investigating AI writing tells and how to eliminate them. The research target is human writers who want to stop their prose sounding like it came out of a language model.

## What it's for

Use this when you want authoritative, evidence-grounded research on what makes text read as AI-generated — at the lexical, structural, rhetorical, tonal, and cognitive levels — and what practical techniques writers can use to audit and fix those patterns in their own drafts.

This is not an AI-detection prompt. It is a writing-craft prompt. The output should help a human writer develop the habit of catching and rewriting AI-adjacent patterns before publishing.

## How to use it

Paste `PROMPT.md` directly into a deep research tool (ChatGPT Deep Research, Gemini Deep Research, Perplexity, etc.) as the research prompt. No modification required for general use.

Optional customisations before pasting:
- Phase 5 can be narrowed to your specific writing format (e.g., long-form technical writing, corporate blog, academic prose) by adjusting the framing sentence.
- The source priority block can be extended with domain-specific writing publications if your context calls for it.

## What to expect

A five-phase research output covering:
1. What the NLP and detection literature says about how LLMs produce text differently from humans
2. A categorised taxonomy of AI tells with concrete examples in each category
3. How skilled readers detect AI text (including the cumulative, hard-to-name effect)
4. Actionable audit and rewriting techniques — including before/after examples
5. A practical pre-publication checklist for analytical and essay writers

Every identified pattern must come with a concrete example. Anecdote is distinguished from empirical evidence. Elimination strategies must be actionable, not aspirational.
