## End-of-Day Wrap-Up

1. Review browsing history and open tabs from today. Identify main topics, projects, and tasks.
2. Compile a summary with these sections:
   - Completed / resolved today
   - In progress / still open
   - Key links or resources to revisit
   - Open questions or blockers
3. Suggest 3 priority tasks for tomorrow based on what's unfinished.
4. Draft a brief end-of-day note (max 5 bullets) ready to send via email or paste to a team channel.
