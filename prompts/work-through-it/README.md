# Work it through

A Socratic thinking-partner prompt for solving or deciding something while doing the
reasoning yourself.

## What it's for

Use this when you want to reach a conclusion but need to think it through — not be
handed an answer. The model follows your thread with one question at a time, reflects
back what it heard, points at holes without smoothing them over, and closes by playing
back the conclusion *you* reached, in your words.

Good for: decisions you're working up to, arguments you want to stress-test, problems
you're too close to think clearly about alone.

## How to use

Paste the system prompt, then describe what you're trying to figure out and what you
already think about it. The model takes it from there.

## What to expect

- One question per turn, always
- Your reasoning reflected back before the next question
- If you're stuck, you get a short list of possibilities to react to (reacting is
  easier than generating from nothing)
- No answer written for you — the closing is a playback of your own thinking
- The model's view only surfaces at the end, only if you ask, clearly labelled as its
  take
