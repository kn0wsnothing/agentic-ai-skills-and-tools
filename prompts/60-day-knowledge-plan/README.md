# 60-day knowledge plan

Generates a complete 60-day immersion plan for any topic: two core books (accessible + technical), curated YouTube resources, complementary study areas, a week-by-week schedule, and a batch of content ideas ready to publish.

## What it's for

Getting up to speed on a topic fast — with enough depth to produce credible content about it. Well-suited for content creators, coaches, consultants, and anyone who needs to go from "interested" to "confident enough to teach" in two months.

## How to use

Paste `PROMPT.md` as the system prompt, then provide your topic in a single message. Example: "Behavioral economics" or "Sleep science" or "Supply chain resilience."

No intake questions — the plan generates in one shot.

## What to expect

A structured plan with:

- **Two books** — one popular/accessible, one technical/academic
- **5-7 YouTube resources** — specific channels or videos with rationale
- **3-5 complementary areas** — adjacent topics that deepen understanding of the main subject
- **Weekly breakdown** — what to focus on each of the 8 weeks, with time estimates
- **Content ideas** — angles and titles you could write, record, or present from this material

Calibrated for 1-2 hours of daily study time.

## Notes

Book and video recommendations are grounded in current sources (last 5 years where possible), with classic works included only when genuinely essential. Treat video links as starting points — verify they're still live and confirm channel credibility before committing time.
