# Junzi ethic — deep research study prompt

A rigorous deep-research prompt for stress-testing a personal Junzi (Confucian superior person) study-practice template against serious scholarship and comparable contemplative traditions.

## What it does

Run this prompt with a deep research tool (ChatGPT Deep Research, Gemini Deep Research, Perplexity Pro, Claude) plus your own study-practice template. The output covers six phases:

1. **Textual-fidelity scan** — does your framing of *junzi* and its practice domains hold up against the *Analects* and Confucian scholarship?
2. **Practice-design scan** — is your daily protocol and weekly review well-designed for character change over 6–24 months?
3. **Comparator deep-dive** — Stoic *prosochē*, Ignatian examen, lectio divina, Mussar, and Zen koan study compared in a structured table.
4. **Synthesis** — cross-traditional patterns, genuine tensions with Confucian formation, risks specific to private English-language practice.
5. **Template recommendations** — concrete, prioritized edits (keep / revise / add / cut).
6. **Tool design recommendations** — what an AI-assisted study skill should and should not do.

## Who it's for

Practitioners who have built or are building a private Confucian character-formation practice in English, without a teacher or lineage, and want rigorous feedback from both textual scholarship and contemplative-practice design.

## How to use

1. Open `PROMPT.md`.
2. In the "Artefact under review" section, paste your full Junzi study-practice template.
3. Name your primary translation so the researcher can account for its interpretive frame.
4. Run with your deep research tool of choice.

## Note

This is a personal-practice prompt. It is designed for one practitioner's specific template; you need to supply your own artefact in the placeholder block before running it. The research question and phases are fully reusable — the artefact block is the only part that requires your own content.
