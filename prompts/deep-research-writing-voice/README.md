# deep-research-writing-voice

A ready-to-run deep research prompt for investigating how writers discover and develop a distinctive personal voice in non-fiction — with a specific focus on using LLMs as generative thinking partners in that process.

## What it's for

Use this when you want a research-grounded answer to the question: how do you actually develop voice? Not in principle, but specifically — what methods have worked, what the craft literature says, and what concrete exercises you can run with an AI partner to surface and codify your own stylistic instincts.

This pairs with the AI writing tells prompt (which is about removing AI-like patterns). This one is about building something distinctively yours.

## How to use it

Paste `PROMPT.md` directly into a deep research tool (ChatGPT Deep Research, Gemini Deep Research, Perplexity, etc.) as the research prompt.

Phase 4 — the section on voice for analytical/practitioner writers — is written for a writer with domain expertise who writes for general intelligent audiences. If your profile is different (fiction writer, journalist, academic), replace the Phase 4 framing to match your context.

## What to expect

A four-phase research output covering:
1. What "voice" actually is — definitions from writing coaches, editors, and scholars, and where they diverge
2. Methods and frameworks for voice development, from classic craft approaches to contemporary practice
3. How LLMs can be used generatively in voice development — five exercise categories (voice archaeology, imitation and contrast, constraint-based writing, Socratic dialogue, codification) with actual runnable prompts, not just descriptions
4. Tailored guidance for a writer with analytical instincts and domain expertise who wants their prose to feel like a person thinking, not a report being filed

The quality checks enforce specificity: every exercise must be runnable, every admired writer named must be explained, and contested territory (whether LLMs help or hinder voice development) must be represented honestly.
