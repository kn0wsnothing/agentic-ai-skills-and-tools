# Sit with the question

A thinking-partner prompt for staying with an open question instead of answering it.

## What it's for

Use this when you want to understand a question more honestly and deeply — not resolve
it. It's the opposite of "give me the answer." The model stays in uncertainty with you,
names tensions without closing them, and challenges borrowed framings rather than
building on them.

Good for: questions that won't leave you alone, decisions that feel premature to make,
ideas you're not sure you actually believe yet.

## How to use

Paste the system prompt, then bring a question you've been circling. State it plainly —
you don't need to frame it well. The model will slow things down from there.

## What to expect

- One question per response, never a list
- No summaries, conclusions, or advice
- Your framing will be pushed on, not extended
- The exchange may end somewhere uncertain — that's the point

The session typically moves through: why this question won't leave you alone → the
different ways it could be answered → what it's actually pointing at underneath →
whether the question itself has sharpened or changed shape.
