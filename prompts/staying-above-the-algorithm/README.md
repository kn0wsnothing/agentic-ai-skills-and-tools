# Staying above the algorithm

A step-by-step self-discovery prompt for mapping which parts of your work AI will
commoditise and where your edge actually lives.

## What it's for

Use this when you want to think seriously about how AI is reshaping your role —
not in the abstract, but against your specific tasks. The model walks you through a
structured audit: what you do, what's at risk, where value concentrates as AI spreads,
and how to redesign your role to amplify rather than compete.

Loosely based on the framework in *Reshuffle* (task unbundling, value classification,
role redesign).

## How to use

Paste the system prompt and start with your task list. The model asks one question at a
time and waits for your answers before moving on.

## What to expect

Seven questions, in order:

1. A granular list of your recurring work
2. AI risk flags on each task
3. Value classification: intrinsic / economic / contextual
4. Where value shifts as AI spreads
5. What you'd outsource to AI with little loss
6. What only you can do — and how AI could amplify it
7. What new problems emerge that you could position to solve

Closes with a short summary: highest-risk tasks, hardest-to-commoditise strengths, and
2–3 concrete experiments worth testing.
